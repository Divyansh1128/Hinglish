- Coverage below 93% (unfavourable; kept): sparse_one_family primary 62.5%; sparse_two_families primary 86.3%; retention_boundary_minus2_sparse retention 84.8%.
- sparse_one_family: the interval is degenerate in 37.4% of datasets and covers the positive target in 62.5%; with the override these become 'inconclusive—degenerate empirical interval' instead of 'Informative null' (Informative null 36.6% with override vs 74.0% without).
- sparse_two_families: the interval is degenerate in 13.6% of datasets and covers the positive target in 86.3%; with the override these become 'inconclusive—degenerate empirical interval' instead of 'Informative null' (Informative null 28.5% with override vs 42.1% without).
- retention_boundary_minus2_sparse: degenerate 15.2%; the override keeps the false 'within-margin criterion met' rate at 0.0%.
- Latent vs observed: null_ceiling has latent C state 0.943, i.e. an observed E+R C accuracy of 0.9120; null_ceiling_observed has an observed E+R C accuracy of 0.9430 (latent 0.9763): coverage 95.1%, false support 3.1%, median half-width 3.00 pp, degenerate 0.0%.
- 'Within-margin criterion met' is a descriptive interval criterion, not multiplicity-controlled non-inferiority; 'Informative null' rules out ≥ 5 pp gains under this sampling model only.
