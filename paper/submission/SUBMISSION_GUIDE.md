# EACL 2027 Student Research Workshop: submission guide

Official call (check before submitting; dates can change): https://2027.eacl.org/calls/srw/

| Date (AoE) | What |
|---|---|
| **6 Nov 2026** | Optional mentorship draft (must also be anonymous) |
| 5 Dec 2026 | Mentor feedback |
| **15 Dec 2026** | Direct submission deadline |
| 5 Jan 2027 | Notification |
| 19 Jan 2027 | Camera-ready |

## Files in this folder
- `EACL2027_SRW_submission.pdf`: the anonymous 4-page short paper (ACL review format, line numbers). Upload this.
- `metadata.md`: title, abstract, keywords and TL;DR, ready to paste into the form.
- `responsible_nlp_checklist.md`: draft checklist answers. Fill the 3 CONFIRM items honestly.
- `supplementary_anonymous.zip`: anonymous code, data and results (1.7 MB unzipped; held-out test data withheld). Upload it as supplementary material if the form allows it. Never paste a GitHub link in the paper or form.

## Steps
1. Open the submission system linked from the SRW call and create or log in to your account (OpenReview profiles take time to be approved; do this early).
2. Choose the **Student Research Workshop** track and **short paper**.
3. Enter **all authors** (you as student first author; optionally your faculty guide) **only in the form**, never in the PDF.
4. Paste the title, abstract and keywords from `metadata.md`.
5. Choose **archival** (appears in the ACL Anthology, so it counts as a publication) or **non-archival** (you could later publish the same results elsewhere). If you want this to count as a published paper, choose archival.
6. Fill the Responsible NLP checklist from `responsible_nlp_checklist.md`.
7. Upload the PDF and the supplementary ZIP, then submit before 15 Dec, 23:59 AoE.

## Before you press submit
- Open the PDF once: no names, no DTU, no GitHub (verified when built, but check again if you edit).
- Consider making the GitHub repository private until reviews are back.
- If you change any text, rebuild in `paper/latex/` with `tectonic main.tex`, check that the main text still ends on page 4, and copy the PDF here again.
