# Quick re-check of 5 repaired problems (about 10 minutes)

The adjudicator suggested better wording for a few texts you reviewed earlier. Please check each **new** sentence on your own, without discussing it with the other reviewer.

Open your CSV (`R01_rereview.csv` or `R02_rereview.csv`) in Google Sheets or Excel. Each row shows the current text, the proposed new text, and the English version for comparison. Fill in:
- **still_same_meaning_as_E**: yes or no. Same numbers, same question, nothing added or lost?
- **naturalness_new**: 1–5. How natural the new sentence sounds.
- **decision**: ACCEPT_REPAIR if the new text is good, REJECT_REPAIR if not; say why in notes.

Then download as CSV and send it back to the coordinator.
