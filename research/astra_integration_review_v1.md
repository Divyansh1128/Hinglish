## Main issue found and resolved

The first closest-work draft defined an exact comparator to require paired seeds and extensive split/reporting controls. That risked describing substantive prior E+R/E+C constant-English-target work as absent merely because its rigor/reporting differed. I recommended separating the **core intervention** from budget/split evidence and replication/reporting quality. The coordinating agent reports this distinction has been added to the closest-work section and specification opening.

This matters for the paper: absent seed reporting, an unrecorded family ledger or another checkpoint policy is a limitation or replication opportunity, not enough to claim a new core training question. Keep that distinction when integrating future sources. I did not independently reopen every primary paper; the literature specialist's source-based conclusions still require the coordinating agent's evidence checks.

## Clarifications worth retaining in the final specification

1. Pilot difficulty may inform **prospective construction criteria/strata** for new confirmatory data. It must not select final items by their individual model correctness or reuse pilot families. The specification already says this; interpret the validity document's caution about selecting by pilot difficulty consistently with that distinction.
2. A seed-averaged formal test is still an open gate. Single-pair McNemar planning cannot certify the new primary test. Resolve population, conditional claim, resampling/test assumptions and calibration before preregistration; the present proposal properly does not claim this is done.
3. A repeated false-format failure in the reviewed pilot does not license silent scorer broadening. The strict endpoint and separately versioned mathematical audit should both remain visible. If development shows measurement failure, repair/version a later protocol rather than erasing the original run.
4. The full real-browser reviewer workflow is still unverified; perform a disposable software-only download/import/resume exercise before people invest in all 90 questions. This does not populate human approval.
5. Source terms, release permissions, independent authors, final N and training-family count remain pending. Their absence is appropriate in a planning phase but must not disappear from the later confirmation gate.
