# Datasets and data sourcing for Phase 2 (beyond 30 families)

Claude deep-research vertical, 15 October 2026. Sources were accessed on 15 October 2026. This is desk research only. I downloaded no datasets, ran no models and changed no project files. "Unverified" means I could not confirm the point from a primary page. Effort figures are planning assumptions taken from [the sampling plan](../sampling_and_annotation_plan_2026-10-11.md). They are not measurements.

**Bottom line.** No existing dataset provides human-authored, licence-clear and contamination-free **E/R/C (all Roman-script) parallel** math word problems. Every Hindi or Hinglish math resource found is derived from GSM8K or MATH, or is HAWP, which has no visible licence and is partly translated from public English benchmarks. Phase-2 FINAL_TEST and DEV families should therefore be **newly authored**. Existing datasets are useful for three things only:
- licence-clear supplementary TRAIN material, with a flagged contamination confound;
- spelling and style references for R;
- sanity checks outside the study.

## 2. What may seed Phase 2, and what is a contamination risk

**Licence view.** This is not legal advice. Each resource falls into one of three tiers.
- **Permitted with attribution:** GSM8K (MIT), GSM8K-Hi (CC-BY-4.0), bingbangboom/gsm8k-hindi (MIT tag) and CodeMixBench (Apache-2.0 card). Derivative renderings are allowed if the notices are kept. MGSM's licence is ambiguous and it has no Hindi anyway.
- **Do not redistribute without permission:** HAWP (no licence, so default copyright applies), Anand data (no licence; release incomplete), sarvamai/gsm8k-indic (licence unstated) and Indi-RomCoM (not released). Internal reading as a style reference is lower-risk than derivation. Seek written permission before building any released family on them.
- **Tooling:** [GSM-Symbolic](https://github.com/apple/ml-gsm-symbolic) templates come under an Apple custom software licence ([LICENSE](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/apple/ml-gsm-symbolic/main/LICENSE)) that allows modification and redistribution with notice. The templates themselves are derived from GSM8K test, though.

**Contamination view, for Qwen3-4B.**
- Every GSM8K-derived resource shares GSM8K's mathematical skeletons: MGSM, GSM8K-Hi, gsm8k-indic, CM-GSM8K, CodeMixTax, Indi-RomCoM, bingbangboom and GSM-Symbolic.
- Translation and romanization preserve numbers and structure, so a model that memorised the English item may recognise it.
- The [Qwen3 report](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.09388) lists GSM8K and MGSM among its evaluated benchmarks. It also says the 36T-token pretraining data includes Qwen2.5-Math-generated synthetic Q&A. I found **no decontamination statement in the part I read; this is unverified**.
- [GSM1k (Zhang et al. 2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.00332) found accuracy drops of up to 8 points on fresh GSM8K-style items for some model families. This is evidence that GSM8K memorisation occurs. That page does not name Qwen specifically.
- HAWP's translated part comes from public English sets (ASDiv, etc.).

**Classify all of these as contaminated for DEV and FINAL_TEST.**

**Use in TRAIN is a design decision with a specific confound.** Contamination might seem harmless in training because it hits all three arms equally. But a memorised problem may be *more recognisable in E than in R or C*, so E+E could get a "free" lift. That interacts with the primary E+C vs E+R contrast only weakly, but it does interact with English retention. If GSM8K-train-derived items are used:
- keep them as a separately tagged TRAIN stratum;
- report a sensitivity analysis without them;
- never take items from GSM8K **test**, because those feed every Hindi test set above.

**Ancestry.** Per the [spec §family ledgers](../astra_experiment_spec_v1.md), translations, paraphrases and numerical siblings inherit family ancestry. Consequences:
- **A template with 50 numeric instances is one family, not 50.** Templating improves rendering consistency and gold computation, but it does not create independent families.
- All 30 pilot families and their descendants stay excluded.
- New families must not be numerical siblings of pilot items or of each other.

**Public-repo leak.** The project repo is publicly indexed. Pinned Qwen3-4B weights cannot absorb it. The risks are blind review and future reuse of the data. Keep the gold for FINAL_TEST and DEV out of public pushes until freeze, consistent with the existing no-gold-leak rule.

## 3. Authoring options and tooling

| Option | What it does | Strengths | Weaknesses / risks | Tooling (verified licence) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Human spec → template → renderings** | A human writes a structured family spec: quantities, operations, unit, gold computed by code, topic and operation-vocabulary tags. E/R/C are drafted from the spec | Gold is computed rather than typed. Matched structure across forms. Operation-word tags plug into the [vocabulary covariate](../operation_vocabulary_2026-10-15.md) | Spec authoring is the bottleneck. Template phrasing can become formulaic and reduce naturalness. One template = one family | Own code. [GSM-Symbolic](https://github.com/apple/ml-gsm-symbolic) as a methods reference only (contaminated source) |
| **B. LLM drafting + human verification** | An open-weight model drafts E (or R/C from E). Humans solve blind and rate equivalence and naturalness | Fastest per family. Already the pilot workflow | Drafting-model style may favour or penalise particular forms. Avoid Qwen-family drafters, to keep Qwen's own phrasing out of the test. AI-authorship disclosure is needed. Novelty of the problems is not guaranteed: LLMs regurgitate GSM8K-like items, so run an n-gram/embedding check against GSM8K/MGSM | [Sarvam-M](https://huggingface.co/sarvamai/sarvam-m) (Apache-2.0; 24B, so heavy on a T4). Other open models: check each licence (unverified here). No paid APIs |
| **C. MT pipeline for R: E → Devanagari → Roman** | [IndicTrans2](https://github.com/AI4Bharat/IndicTrans2) en→indic (MIT; 1B or distilled 200M), then [IndicXlit](https://github.com/AI4Bharat/IndicXlit) indic→en romanization (MIT; `ai4bharat-transliteration`) | Deterministic, free and local. Gives a reviewable baseline R | Output is formal (shuddh) Hindi with no borrowing policy. IndicXlit is word-level, so sentences must be split. Natural Roman spellings vary ([Dakshina](https://aclanthology.org/2020.lrec-1.294.pdf)) and every R needs human normalisation. Cannot produce C | Training data: [Aksharantar](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ai4bharat/Aksharantar) (CC-BY for manual data, CC0 for mined data; about 1.31M Hindi pairs). [Sarvam-Translate](https://huggingface.co/sarvamai/sarvam-translate) is GPL-3.0 and has no Romanized output stated |
| **D. Scheme transliteration** | [indic_transliteration](https://github.com/indic-transliteration/indic_transliteration_py) (MIT: ITRANS/HK/IAST/OPTITRANS) or [Aksharamukha](https://github.com/virtualvinodh/aksharamukha) (AGPL-3.0) | Reproducible | Produces scholarly romanization, not chat-style Roman Hindi. CodeMixTax's ITRANS-style HI-ROM illustrates the gap. Aksharamukha's AGPL is a problem if bundled in released code | Avoid for R/C text. Usable only for lexicon lookups |
| **E. Fresh human authoring (GSM1k-style)** | Bilingual annotators write new problems from a style guide, without consulting public benchmarks | Strongest independence and naturalness claim, especially when authors differ from reviewers | Slowest. Needs trained authors and an independence pledge | [GSM1k methodology](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.00332) as precedent. Whether GSM1k itself was released: unverified |
| **F. Adapt licensed Hindi GSM8K** | Romanise GSM8K-Hi (CC-BY) and human-rewrite into R/C | Cheap volume of human-reviewed Hindi | GSM8K **test** ancestry. Contaminated; TRAIN at most, and preferably not even there | — |

## 4. Recommendation: about 300 families in 3 months (mid-Dec 2026 to mid-Mar 2027)

The split is a suggested target and still needs approval: **FINAL_TEST 100, DEV 40, TRAIN 160 accepted families**, plus 20–25% over-generation for attrition. The 16 training-smoke families come out of the TRAIN budget or are built first.

**Strategy 1 (FINAL_TEST + DEV, 140 families): human-written E specs (E), rendered with Option B, under full double-blind review.**
- Process:
  - Bilingual team members write each family's English problem and spec from scratch, using only the style guide and topic/operation quotas, with no benchmark browsing.
  - Code computes the gold.
  - A non-Qwen open model drafts R and C under the frozen borrowing policy.
  - IndicXlit/IndicTrans2 output is available as an R cross-check.
  - Then the existing two-reviewer + adjudicator protocol runs.
- Authors must not review their own families. Run a GSM8K/MGSM similarity screen before review.
- **Effort:** about 70 person-min per candidate (30 authoring + 32 review + 7.6 repair) × 175 candidates (140 at 20% attrition) ≈ **205 person-hours**.

**Strategy 2 (TRAIN, 160 families): template-spec + LLM drafting (A+B) with single-reviewer verification and a 20% double-review audit.**
- TRAIN does not carry the inferential claim, so lighter review is defensible. Still record the audit disagreement rate and freeze before training.
- **Effort:** about 30 person-min per family (10 spec, 14 single-review solve/triplet, 2 adjudication on flags, 4 repair) × 190 candidates ≈ 95 h, plus the 20% audit ≈ 10 h, ≈ **105 person-hours**.

**Strategy 3 (optional TRAIN scale-up, only if Phase 2 needs more than 160 TRAIN families): GSM8K *train*-split derivatives (MIT).**
- Draft R/C from English, with GSM8K-Hi/bingbangboom only as phrasing aids (not test-derived items). Tag the items as a contaminated stratum and report results with and without them.
- **Effort:** about 15–20 min per family with single review.

**Total for Strategies 1+2:** about **310 person-hours**. With 4 contributors over 13 weeks that is about 6 h/person/week. The illustrative figure in the sampling plan is 298 h for 300 families. These numbers are assumptions; recalibrate them with the timings recorded during the pilot review.

**Immediate low-cost actions, before December.**
1. Ask Sarvam on HF about the licence of `gsm8k-indic`. Ask HAWP's authors for a licence. Optional.
2. Prototype the IndicTrans2 → IndicXlit R baseline on 5 English-only toy items. Measure how much human normalisation it needs against the frozen borrowing policy.
3. Write the family-spec schema and author style guide. Include an explicit "no benchmark consultation" rule and pilot-ancestry exclusion checks.
4. Add a similarity screen against GSM8K train/test and MGSM-en to the build scripts.
