Sharing an operation does not prove two items are numerical siblings; adding an operation does not prove independent authoring. Use the family report's signatures alongside actual creation history. The goal is comparable clarity under an agreed borrowing policy, not arbitrary replacement of Hindi terminology with English. Human judgments remain necessary.
