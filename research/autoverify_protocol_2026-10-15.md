# Automated-checker validation and data separation — 15 October 2026

**Status: proposal for task 27, not an executed validation or a protocol adoption.** D027 fixes 70 Pilot-B and 300 Phase-2 families (100 FINAL_TEST / 40 DEV / 160 TRAIN), AI drafting, code-computed gold, Claude/GPT solves, and human coverage. The operational choices below require recorded adoption before use; no returned reviews or checker performance are asserted.
Basis: [decisions, D027](../docs/DECISIONS.md), [dataset research](claude_deep_research/datasets_2026-10-15.md), [experiment specification](astra_experiment_spec_v1.md), and [authoring guide](../authoring/AUTHORING_GUIDE.md). D027 supersedes their older human-authoring/full-double-review scale assumptions. Preserve their lineage, exposure and frozen-pilot constraints.

## 1. Pre-register a family-level validation on the original 30 pilot candidates

- Before opening R01/R02/A01 returns, timestamp and hash this adopted protocol, the 30 original v2 triplets/golds, checker code/configuration, solver prompts, exact model identifiers, decoding settings, task ordering and flag rule. Archive predictions before joining human labels. Register prior AI access to pilot text/gold and known issues; this is a prospective human-label comparison, not an unseen-data benchmark.
- Each solver gets one rendering in a fresh context, with only its identity/form/text; no reference equation, solution, gold, sibling rendering, drafting conversation or other solver response. Claude and GPT independently solve all 90 renderings, even where their model family drafted the text. Never use Qwen as drafter, solver or checker. Separate contexts reduce information sharing but do not make model errors statistically independent.
- Define a positive prediction as **any family flag**: solver–gold mismatch, solver disagreement, cross-form quantity mismatch, ambiguity note, missing/unparseable output, unit incompatibility, incompatible requested unknown/entity relation, or failed structural/gold/lineage screen. Freeze the exact implemented mapping, including semantic-comparison rules, before validation; an unimplemented rule is not a tested capability. Exact rational comparison uses frozen unit aliases/conversions, never a numeric tolerance.
- Keep separate automated reason codes and raw extracts; matching quantity multisets and answers cannot establish equivalent relations. A schema failure or missing solve remains a flag, not an excluded denominator. No answer-dependent solver retries; log any infrastructure retry under the same frozen configuration.
- **Ground truth is A01's adjudicated disposition of the original v2 family before repair**, based on both reviewers' immutable independent stage1 solves and stage2 judgments, with reasons and text hashes. Map packet `EXCLUDE` to `REJECT`; positive = REVISE or REJECT for any reason, including naturalness, readability, classification or ancestry. Negative = ACCEPT unchanged. Reviewer votes alone and eventual repaired ACCEPT status are not ground truth.
- A01 records the original disposition without seeing automated predictions or Qwen outputs. If only a repaired decision exists, obtain an explicit retrospective original-version adjudication and label it retrospective. Unresolved decisions, invalid solve-before-gold attestations or missing reviews prevent a complete validation; show missing counts rather than invent labels. Exposure must be disclosed and, where necessary, an eligible unexposed reviewer obtained.
- Compute TP = flagged human-positive; FP = flagged human-negative; FN = unflagged human-positive; TN = unflagged human-negative. Report all four counts, precision TP/(TP+FP), recall TP/(TP+FN), specificity, negative predictive value, flag rate and 95% Wilson intervals at the **family** level (N=30, not 90). Zero denominators are undefined, never perfect performance.
- Primary precision/recall include every human rejection reason. Also show descriptive reason-specific results and a content-only diagnostic, without substituting either for the primary gate. List every FN, distinguishing wrong gold, changed mathematical meaning/unknown/unit and lineage leakage as critical from language-quality failures; overlapping reasons are allowed.
- **Proposed gate:** all 30 labels resolved, at least 10 positive and 10 negative families, precision >=0.70, recall >=0.90, and zero critical FN. These are operational point-estimate thresholds, not a claim that population recall exceeds 0.90; publish intervals even if wide. Too few positive/negative cases makes the gate inconclusive. No lowering thresholds after seeing labels.
- Passing permits only the bounded human-triage workflow below for the unchanged checker version; FINAL_TEST still receives full human review. Failure or inconclusiveness means full primary human review of Pilot-B/Phase-2 pending a new gate. Freeze any revised checker and validate on a fresh, fully reviewed exclusion cohort; reused pilot labels are development evidence. Freeze that cohort's size/rules before labels, never add cases until a passing result appears.
- Treat any critical FN found in later random audits as suspension of triage: quarantine the affected version's unreviewed families, complete human review, repair and recheck before release/use. Report the failure and all original metrics. Model, prompt, comparison-rule or borrowing-policy changes require a new validation/version, not inherited approval.

## 2. Drafter split and what the paper can claim

- GPT drafts the mathematical specification and all E/R/C texts for FINAL_TEST and DEV; Claude drafts TRAIN. Keep independent sessions and separate input folders; share only the frozen topic, operation, borrowing and difficulty guidelines. Claude may draft Pilot-B as already assigned, but its items and descendants never seed Phase 2.
- Code computes exact gold from the specification; solvers independently solve the text. Agreement with code does not establish that the specification matches the question. Human edits retain `AI_DRAFT` provenance and per-form original model/prompt/version records.
- Optional subset: reserve **12 of the 100 FINAL_TEST slots**, two per topic, for entirely human-written specifications and E/R/C texts, before drafting begins. Use a bilingual author other than their reviewer; if unavailable, omit the subset rather than relabel AI-edited items. It replaces 12 GPT families, does not increase N, and all 12 undergo independent human review plus both AI solves.
- Report results separately for those 12 and the 88 GPT families, plus the prespecified 100-family aggregate; the small human stratum supports descriptive sensitivity only. If any form was AI-drafted, classify the family as AI-assisted instead of human-written.
- GPT-held-out versus Claude-TRAIN probes transfer across these drafting systems, but author style is confounded with split and is not randomized. GPT DEV also exposes the held-out author style during permitted tuning. Shared solver families can perpetuate shared errors; fresh contexts do not eliminate self-verification bias or memorized benchmark templates.
- Disclose model identifiers/dates, prompts, settings, drafting/edit counts, which model drafted and checked each split, code-gold method, human coverage, exposure, rejected/repaired counts, optional-subset selection and any public disclosure. Claim performance on curated synthetic data; do not claim natural human Hinglish, contamination freedom, or general author-independent transfer from this split alone.

## 3. Sampling and the second human reviewer

- Freeze a complete candidate-wave roster and its first-pass checker reports before human selection or edits. Give each candidate a coordinator-assigned immutable ID before drafting. Sampling unit is a family, and selection includes every E/R/C rendering. No convenience replacements or sampling based on Qwen correctness.
- Reproducible selection: rank eligible IDs by ascending SHA-256 of UTF-8 `20261015|purpose|split|wave|family_id`, tie-break by ID; take the first k without replacement. Freeze the literal seed, purpose strings (`primary20`, `double30`, `topup20`), split/wave names, roster hash and selected IDs. This is the prespecified pseudorandom rule; do not reroll a seed or renumber IDs.
- Primary review = **all flagged families plus ceil(0.20 × U) of the U unflagged families in each Pilot-B, DEV and TRAIN wave**. This interprets D027's 20% sample as a sample of unflagged families, matching task 26; flagged cases are a separate census. With no flags and target-sized rosters the samples are 14/8/32 respectively. FINAL_TEST: all 100, regardless of flags; review all additional candidates submitted as replacements too.
- Reviewers first solve forms independently without gold, flags or other decisions; then compare triplets and rate classification, equivalence, ambiguity, naturalness and readability. Show gold and reasons only after first judgments are saved. A gold column beside the questions is not a blind review; use a separate delayed key. ACCEPT requires no unresolved issues and all naturalness/readability ratings >=4/5; otherwise REVISE/REJECT with reasons.
- For **30 additional double-reviewed families**, select by `double30` ranking from the first frozen candidate wave: 10 Pilot-B, 10 FINAL_TEST, 5 DEV, 5 TRAIN, independently of flags or primary selection. Add selected families to primary coverage where necessary. If any roster is smaller than its allocation, wait for the planned roster; do not silently substitute another split. Retain rejected cases in agreement analysis; no replacement with easier cases.
- A second bilingual human independently completes the same first-pass rubric, blind to primary judgments, flags and gold until solving. No person reviews their own authored family. Save both raw decisions before discussion; resolve disagreements through documented consensus or a distinct adjudicator where available. Unresolved cases stay excluded; do not invent an A01 role or adjudication that did not occur.
- Report the 3×3 ACCEPT/REVISE/REJECT confusion table, raw percent agreement and unweighted Cohen's kappa; also binary ACCEPT versus REVISE/REJECT agreement/kappa. Report exact canonical answer+unit agreement by form and naturalness/readability exact agreement plus linearly weighted kappa on the 1–5 ratings, separately by form. Include category marginals, missing counts, and undefined kappa when a marginal is degenerate.
- Give 95% percentile intervals from 10,000 family bootstrap replicates, seed 20261016, resampling within the four split allocations and retaining both raters/all forms together; state undefined replicate counts. N=30 provides imprecise agreement estimates, not 90 independent items. Report allocation and split-specific counts; the pooled agreement describes this audit mix, not a population-weighted 370-family estimate.
- Estimate residual unflagged REVISE/REJECT frequency only from the initial `primary20` random selections, with their original-version labels and denominators; report flags separately and do not pool the enriched review set as a prevalence estimate. Double-review and top-up selections are reported separately. Preserve all initial selections through repairs/rejections.
- Revisions retain family ancestry, get new text/gold hashes and both solver checks, and receive human re-review of the whole triplet; double-reviewed revisions need both reviewers again. Replacements are new unrelated candidates in the same split/topic under a new wave and the same sampling rules. Before freeze, ensure at least ceil(0.20 × retained initially-unflagged families) were randomly selected for human review per non-test split; use `topup20` ranking on remaining eligible families if attrition reduced coverage, repeating after further rejections. All ever-flagged retained families must be human-reviewed.

## 5. Limits and implementation boundary

AI drafting plus AI checking can share blind spots; executable arithmetic only checks the encoded calculation. Thirty pilot families are a small, previously seen validation set and may not represent new authors/styles. Passing the gate supports monitored triage, not a certified defect rate or semantic equivalence guarantee.
One primary bilingual reviewer creates systematic preference and error risks; a 30-family second-review audit measures limited agreement, not correctness. **Naturalness is human-only:** no automated pass certifies it. Unreviewed families must be labelled auto-screened with naturalness unassessed; claims of human-validated naturalness apply only to the reviewed subset. Full naturalness validation would require full human review.
Use explicit per-family review coverage in any new cohort format. Do not set legacy `test1-1` two-reviewer/adjudicator attestations for single-reviewed or auto-screened families, or weaken the unchanged 30-family pilot validator. Sol's task-26 draft tools require integration review against this proposal (flag coverage, sampling, delayed gold and versioned review status); this document changes no code or data. Any such implementation/adoption is a later recorded action.
